# Validation data notice

This package is an eight-ingredient **validation fixture**, not the production catalog.

USDA Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, SR Legacy April 2018. Selected factual food IDs and descriptions retrieved 2026-10-08 from the official CSV archive. FoodData Central dedicates its data to the public domain under CC0; attribution is requested and retained. See https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/ and the source manifest. No USDA images are included. No nutrient data was imported.

The project-authored factual Turkish/English labels, aliases, culinary categories, stable keys and preferred-unit choices in this validation package are dedicated to CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This dedication concerns these data files only; it does not change the license of application code. These labels are curation, not USDA translations or nutritional endorsements.

Mass and volume listed together indicate supported dimensions; they do **not** authorize conversion without verified density. Missing observations mean unknown. In particular, missing allergens never mean allergen-free.

Whole milk is the specified unfortified 3.25% product form, coffee is brewed, eggs are raw whole eggs, and lime is raw. Granulated sugar, powdered sugar and future sugar syrup remain distinct identities. Generic culinary forms are not branded products.
