# Production catalog notices — version 2

235 generic culinary identities selected from USDA Agricultural Research Service,
FoodData Central, SR Legacy April 2018. Original English descriptors and FDC IDs
are retained unchanged in usda-records.json. USDA factual data is public domain,
provided under CC0: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/#licensing . Attribution is
retained as requested. No third-party images or branded-product database included.

The project dedicates the authored factual labels, aliases, category assignments
and selection in this directory to CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . This dedication concerns these
catalog data files only and does not alter the software's licensing.

Turkish and simplified English labels are project-authored semantic curation,
not USDA-supplied translations or independently certified terminology. Source
qualifiers are retained; ambiguous regional mappings are omitted. Observations
for nutrition, density, allergens and ABV are absent, not zero or allergen-free.
US proof appears only where explicitly stated in the source's identity; it does
not establish an application ABV observation or final drink strength.

Production dataset version 2 contains 227 additional identities and eight
re-reviewed shared identities from validation version 1. The validation package
is separate, unchanged and not bundled for automatic installation. Its stable
IDs are retained, preventing duplicates on upgrade. The source snapshot version
2018-04-selection-2 describes our immutable extraction, not a new USDA release.
