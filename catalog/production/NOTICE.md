# Production catalog notices — version 5

508 canonical culinary identities: 454 selected USDA SR Legacy April 2018
identities and 54 original project-authored reference-backed identities.
The USDA source facts and IDs remain unchanged in usda-records.json; factual
data is CC0: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/#licensing . USDA Agricultural
Research Service attribution is retained. No images or branded-product dump.

The project dedicates its own labels, aliases, original factual identity entries,
classification and selection to CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Software licensing unchanged.
This dedication does not relicense referenced websites, government documents,
IBA recipes, photographs or third-party databases. Those are not redistributed.
Reference-curation records preserve individual primary URLs in provenance.
Original factual names/classifications were prepared from narrow identity checks;
no recipe instructions, quantities, regulatory definitions or health claims copied.

Turkish and English labels are project curation, not USDA translations or
independently certified terminology. All 508 were structurally audited; three
Turkish display names were explicitly selected by the user and 15 questions
remain for culinary review. Unknown nutrition,
density, allergens and ABV are absent. Regulatory minimum strengths are never
actual ingredient ABV defaults, and non-alcoholic naming never implies 0% ABV.

Version 4 adds 26 identities to the archived 482-record version 3. All old
canonical IDs are retained. Eight shared validation identities are not added
again to production counts. The separate validation package is unchanged and
not installed automatically. Source snapshots 2018-04-selection-4 and production-3
are project extraction/curation versions, not new upstream USDA releases.

Version 5 changes only the Turkish display names for stable keys
`usda-sr-170918`, `usda-sr-168921` and `usda-sr-168588`. It adds no ingredient,
alias, category, factual observation or USDA descriptor. The user-selected
wording is recorded separately as `recipeatlas-terminology/user-decisions-1`;
prior curation snapshots remain intact. The exact version 4 package is archived
under `catalog/releases/4` for upgrade and historical verification.
