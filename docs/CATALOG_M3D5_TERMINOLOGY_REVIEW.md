# M3D-5 beverage terminology and identity review

The 59 new Turkish and English labels are **project editorial curation**, not independently approved translations. Source descriptors, pinned QID revisions and authored labels remain separate in the manifest and provenance rows. The structural audit found no missing bilingual labels or exact name/alias collisions. That automated result does not prove culinary synonymy or linguistic quality.

The 16 deferred M3D-5 candidates and their individual reasons are in [the machine-readable decision ledger](../catalog/preparation/m3d5/candidates.json). Priority review groups:

- **Drink identity:** Wikidata `Q192892` currently has an anomalous ayran description mentioning vodka. USDA `174863` groups malt beverages with non-alcoholic beer. Neither is a safe basis for an alcohol classification.
- **Bar formulations:** Wikidata `Q26877308` and `Q26877423` have insufficient descriptions for precise vermouth distinctions; `Q134967538`, `Q134967545` and `Q27670710` lack useful label/form evidence for vanilla, ginger and passion-fruit syrup. USDA `167957` may overlap generic fruit syrup.
- **Botanical and prepared forms:** `Q140606571` spans more than one chamomile species; `Q28208975` is prepared butterfly-pea tea, not dry flowers. USDA `171882` needs accurate shellfish terminology; `168123` and `168212` are composition-specific drink blends.
- **Wine and malt:** USDA `173176` and `175112` need clear Turkish dessert-wine terminology. Wikidata `Q3485291` may overlap existing malt syrup.

The 10 EXISTING candidates remain represented by their prior canonical identities; no aliases or duplicate ingredient IDs were created. Release 11 did not resolve the three M3C-5 terminology questions, the four M3D-1 review candidates, or later historical deferrals. No human-decision field is implied by autonomous editorial acceptance.

Watchpoints for subsequent review: “Falernum syrup” is represented from a syrup-specific source entity with alcohol status unknown; “ginger beer” is a mixer with alcohol status unknown; “cachaça” is a generic distilled-spirit identity without product-origin certification or ABV; and salep flour is distinct from the existing prepared salep beverage. Presentation overrides remain user-owned and take precedence over canonical labels.
