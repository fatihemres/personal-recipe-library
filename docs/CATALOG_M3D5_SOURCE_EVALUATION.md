The candidate ledger is `catalog/preparation/m3d5/candidates.json` and the editorial input is `selection.tsv`. It records 85 screened candidates: **59 ACCEPT, 16 DEFER, 10 EXISTING, 0 REJECT**. Five proposed USDA records were already in Release 10 and were reclassified EXISTING before production generation. Deferred items are not bundled. The 16 deferrals cover uncertain alcohol status, product-form overlap, botanical identity and inadequately described vermouth/syrup concepts. The historical seven review questions and prior deferred candidates remain separate and unresolved.

The production manifest carries new source IDs, versions and artifact hashes. A source/version pair cannot be reused with different evidence bytes; the importer rejects `CATALOG_SOURCE_CHANGED` transactionally. The original ZIP remains an external build-time input, while the selected source facts and revisions are committed for reproducible offline builds. `python3 tools/prepare_m3d5_candidates.py --archive /path/to/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip --check` independently re-extracts USDA descriptors; ordinary CI checks the pinned extracts and snapshots without downloading data.
