# M3D-5 Release 11 quality audit

Release 10 remains archived at `catalog/releases/10` with its original manifest SHA-256 `c909568c3d4a3487bde706042ba303455524f816dc620d64e5eed32c5193df87`. Release 11 adds **59 beverage-oriented canonical identities** (27 USDA SR Legacy, 32 pinned Wikidata) after evaluating 85 candidates: 59 ACCEPT, 16 DEFER, 10 EXISTING, 0 REJECT. The package has **854 identities: 666 food and 188 beverage**, 269 aliases and 50 category nodes. All 795 historical ingredient objects and the complete category tree compare equal as parsed objects.

`tools/audit_catalog.py` found zero structural errors, duplicate IDs, source-identity conflicts or exact normalized alias collisions. All new identities have stable deterministic IDs, Turkish and English editorial names, two provenance records, a compatible mass or volume default, and no empirical observations. The source/evidence hashes in `catalog/production/manifest.json` match the committed artifacts. The 46 pinned QID snapshot hashes and the USDA archive hash are checked by the preparation tooling. No earlier source/version pair was reused with changed evidence.

The automated report lists 39 possible same-base-label concept groups across the full catalog. They are review signals for qualified culinary forms, not confirmed duplicates, and none was silently merged or deleted. It reported zero capitalization/translation heuristic warnings for this release; that heuristic result is not linguistic certification.

Actual SQLite checks through the Rust importer verified fresh Release-11 installation and direct upgrades from Releases 7, 8, 9 and 10. A wider offline-denied run exercised 29 isolated fresh/historical/repeat opens. Import is idempotent; the new Rust regression tests preserve personal ingredients, recipe references, exact decimal text and units, drafts, favorites and overrides. A deliberately conflicting `wikidata-m3d5` source/version checksum returned `CATALOG_SOURCE_CHANGED` and left the Release-10 database and personal recipe unchanged; removal of only the injected test conflict allowed the upgrade. SQLite integrity and foreign-key checks passed.

The 16 deferred candidates remain outside production, including uncertain vermouth and syrup forms, botanical ambiguity and the anomalous ayran source description. Ten candidates were already represented. The prior three unresolved M3C-5 terminology questions, four M3D-1 review candidates and other historical deferrals remain open. No autonomous label is claimed to have independent human linguistic approval. See `docs/CATALOG_M3D5_TERMINOLOGY_REVIEW.md` and the complete candidate ledger.

This structural audit does not establish the correctness of every translation, alcohol classification or future product-level ABV. No ABV, nutrition, density, allergen or medicinal claim was inferred. Beverage Studio UI and measurement conversion remain out of scope.
